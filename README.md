# Throwing Disc in ONE command block

Minecraft's new Throwing Disc isn't out yet, so here it is early: for Minecraft Java 26.3, made with a single command block. No mods, no datapacks.

## How to use it
1. Make a Java 26.3 world with cheats on (superflat works best). Keep render distance at 8 or more.
2. Give yourself a command block: `/give @s command_block`
3. Open `Throwing_Disc_one_command.txt`, copy everything, and paste it into the command block.
4. Put a button on the side of the command block, face the way you want the range to go, and press it.
5. The command blocks place themselves, and a Disc Forge and a five-stage trick-shot range build in front of you.

## Making a disc
- The chest at the Disc Forge has everything for your first disc: a music disc, a copper ingot and a Disc Upgrade Template.
- Drop the music disc, the copper ingot and the template on the smithing table. You get a **Copper Throwing Disc**.
- To upgrade, drop your disc, a better ingot (or a diamond) and a template on the **anvil**. The anvil doesn't take damage.
- Tiers: copper, iron, gold, diamond, netherite. Better discs hit harder, fly further and bounce more.

## Throwing
- Right-click to throw it. It flies out spinning and comes back to your hand.
- It ricochets off walls, goes through mobs (hitting every one in a line, like Piercing) and smashes glass.
- It works from either hand, so you can have two in the air at once.

## The trick-shot range
Each stage you clear gives you a Disc Upgrade Template and the next ingot.
1. Hit the target.
2. The target is behind a wall, so bank it off the side.
3. Hit all three husks in one throw.
4. Zigzag around two walls.
5. Hit both targets with one throw. Clear it to become a **TRICK SHOT MASTER**.
